# Route report

Generated by `tools/build_routes.js`. Do not edit.

One route per race, planned by `tools/planner/plan.js` and replayed step by step by `tools/planner/validate.js`.
XP and times are estimates (`tools/lib/xp.js`, `tools/lib/travel.js`, `tools/planner/tuning.js`): they pace the
route, the game decides the rest. A grind step means no quest was left for the level.
A hand-in left behind is a step that moves the player more than LEAVE_YARDS while a quest ready to hand in waits
within READY_YARDS of them (`tools/planner/leftbehind.js`, `tools/planner/tuning.js`).

## Before and after

Before: the routes of commit `37a4f8c`, the last ones of the first planner, as `tools/baseline.js` measured them
(`tools/planner/baseline.json`). After: the routes in `NoirGuides/Routes/` now. Both are measured by `tools/planner/measure.js`:

- **Zone changes:** how often the route moves its work to another zone: two steps in a row that do something in
  a zone (accept, objective, turn-in, grind, bind) and name different zones. Steps of the way (travel, flight,
  transport, hearth, learning a flight point) are not counted: the old routes had no such steps.
- **Travel time:** every walk between two steps, every flight, transport and hearth. The new routes are timed as
  written. The old routes name no way at all, so each got the fastest way `tools/lib/travel.js` finds between two
  steps, with every transport open to it: the comparison errs in favour of the old routes.
- **Walked:** the yards of all that on foot, through the passes between zones; the old routes on the same way.
- **Steps, quests, grind steps:** before, as the old report gave them.
